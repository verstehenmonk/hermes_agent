## Mission

One sentence. What does this company exist to do?

## Product

One paragraph. What does it actually do, in plain English, for a buyer who
has never heard of it?

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

### Primary segment
- **Company stage**: e.g. seed–series-B
- **Company size**: e.g. 20–500 employees
- **Industry**: e.g. fintech, dev tools, healthtech
- **Buyer persona**: e.g. Head of Data, VP Engineering
- **Trigger**: what just happened in their life that makes them open to
  buying this? e.g. "just raised a round and needs to operationalize
  analytics"
- **Pain we solve**: the one sentence they'd say to a peer

### Secondary segment
(Same shape. Or delete if you only have one.)

### Anti-ICP
Who is NOT a fit, no matter how nice the conversation. Sales should decline
politely. Examples: companies under N employees, regulated industries you
don't yet support, prospects looking for free tools.
